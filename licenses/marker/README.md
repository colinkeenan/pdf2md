# Licences of the page reader for scanned books

A scanned book (pictures of pages, no text) is read by **marker**
(<https://github.com/datalab-to/marker>, version 1.10.2) with the models of
**surya** (<https://github.com/datalab-to/surya>). pdf2md does not contain
them: it runs marker as a separate program that you install yourself (see
the main README). Nothing is sent anywhere; the pages are read on your own
computer. Whoever runs marker is bound by its licences, whatever the
licence of pdf2md says.

Two licences apply, and both are in this folder in full:

* `LICENSE`: the **code** of marker and surya, the GNU General Public

* `MODEL_LICENSE`: the **model weights**, the "AI Pubs Open RAIL-M License
  (Modified)". In short, and only as a pointer to the text itself:
  * free for personal use and for research;
  * not for any purpose (other than personal use or research) if you, your
    employer or the organization you belong to made more than 2 million US
    dollars in gross revenue in the past year, or has raised more than
    2 million US dollars in funding; and not at all if you or they offer a
    product or service that competes with the licensor's (Datalab);
  * use restrictions (Attachment A), among them: nothing unlawful;
  * its paragraphs 6 to 8 are about what the model writes (the "Output"):
    attribution, and the licence's terms carried on to it.

This summary is not legal advice and leaves things out. Read the two texts
and decide for yourself; if you do not agree with them, do not install
marker (books that carry their text convert without it).
